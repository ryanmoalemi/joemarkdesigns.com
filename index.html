01 — Services

Permit-ready plans for the work people actually build

Six kinds of residential drawings, in plain language. Custom home plans and other residential drafting are available too — describe the project when you call or email.

  • Home Additions

    A new room, a bump-out, or an extra wing. The plan shows the existing house, the proposed addition, and the dimensions, doors, and windows a permit set needs.

  • ADU & Garage Conversions

    Plans to turn a garage into living space, or to draw an accessory dwelling. Kitchen, bath, sleeping area, living area, and the egress the layout needs.

  • Full Remodel Plans

    Reconfigured floor plans for a whole-house remodel, including open kitchen and living layouts. Walls to remove or keep are marked so the permit set is easy to read.

  • Basement Remodels

    Finished-basement drawings with the room layout, bath, egress, and dimensions, ready to submit for a building permit.

  • Kitchen & Bathroom Vanity Remodel Plans

    Permit-ready layouts for kitchen renovations, cabinetry and island layouts, and bathroom and vanity remodels.

  • Retaining Walls

    Site and retaining wall permit drawings with grades, heights, and layout details.
